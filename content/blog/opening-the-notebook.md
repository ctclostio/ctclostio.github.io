The workshop has a new look, and now it has a notebook, too.

The portfolio is home to the projects: little universes, terminal adventures, and tools that make a tedious job a little easier. This space is for what happens along the way.

## Between the milestones

A project page can tell you what something does. A notebook has room for the smaller things: a decision worth explaining, an experiment that led somewhere unexpected, or a problem that is still waiting for an answer.

That's what belongs here. Updates from the workbench, as the work happens.

## A fresh coat of curiosity

The site now has warm paper, orbital doodles, and illustrations drawn from the projects themselves. The technical notes are still there, tucked behind the roadmaps. There is even a tiny universe you can nudge around.

You can [explore the projects](/#projects), [read the roadmaps](/#case-studies), or [follow the notebook through RSS](/feed.xml).

More notes as the experiments continue.
